⚡ How Smart Auto-Routing Works

  • Automatic Department Resolution: Your student department is automatically logged from your profile.
  • Dynamic Faculty Mapping: The system matches your category and department to find the designated faculty coordinator.
  • Fail-Safe Admin Queue: If no exact rule exists, the grievance is routed to the Central Administration dashboard for manual allocation.
  • Status Notifications: You receive in-app alerts whenever faculty add remarks or change status.
  • Escalation Rights: If unresolved within 72 hours, you or faculty can escalate directly to Dean Student Welfare.
